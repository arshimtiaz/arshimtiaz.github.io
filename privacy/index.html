Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

This policy explains how arshimtiaz.com and its owner-managed Google integration handle information.

Website

This is a personal website containing technical writing, research notes and contact details. It does not provide user accounts, payments or public commenting.

The site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage. It is hosted through GitHub Pages and protected by Cloudflare; these providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser details and request logs in order to deliver and protect the site. You can control non-essential analytics through the cookie notice shown on the site.

If you choose to email the site owner, your message and contact details are processed by your email provider and the recipient's email provider for the purpose of replying to you.

Google Calendar and Tasks integration

A private, owner-managed automation integration may access the owner's Google Calendar and Google Tasks after explicit Google authorisation. It is not offered as a public service and does not grant third parties access to Google data.

The integration uses only the permissions needed to read and create calendar events and tasks for the owner's personal reminder workflow. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's device. Google data is not sold, rented or used for advertising.

The owner can revoke the integration's Google access at any time through their Google Account permissions.
